# Every balance is an account; DPS and FDR sit under a Parent Account

Status: accepted (2026-11-14). Supersedes ADR-0012.

Every place money sits, including bank accounts, MFS accounts, In-Hand Cash, DPS, FDR, Sanchaypatra and investments, is a `savings_accounts` row, and every money movement names the account it leaves and the account it enters (`savings_account_id` for the account the routing acts on, `bank_source_id` for the Funding Account on the other side). Only Payment Accounts (`SAVINGS`, `MFS`, `CASH`) pay Spending, receive income or send transfers. A DPS or FDR must name a `SAVINGS` or `MFS` Parent Account, and a withdrawal from it returns to that parent. Income credits its receiving account with gross − TDS. ADR-0012's per-payment-method wallets are dropped, because they recorded the same money a second time with their own opening balances, could not tell one bank from another, and ignored income. We kept `savings_accounts` rather than introducing a new accounts table so that no existing data has to move. The cost is that the table name no longer describes everything it holds. The old `wallets` and `wallet_transfers` tables are left unused until the owner agrees to drop them.
